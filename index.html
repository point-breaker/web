SANTIAGO BERNABEU

The Santiago Bernabéu Stadium, legendary home of Real Madrid CF since 1947, stands as a crown jewel of world football and a testament to modern engineering. Located in Madrid's vibrant Chamartín district, this iconic venue recently underwent a massive €1.76 billion hyper-modern transformation. The sweeping redesign expanded its seating capacity to 83,186 spectators and completely reimagined the stadium's classic Brutal concrete frame into a futuristic architectural masterpiece wrapped in 7,500 stainless steel slats. More than just a fortress for football matchdays, the state-of-the-art arena features an advanced retractable roof system, an indoor hospitality food hall with close to twenty restaurants, a panoramic 360-degree top-tier skywalk, and an incredible retractable underground pitch that safeguards the grass so the venue can smoothly pivot to host multi-genre concerts and major international sporting events like NFL regular-season games.
